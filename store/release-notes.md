# Release notes

## v1.0.0 — first release

For a first release, "What's new" is really a short intro to what the app is.
Copy the one that fits each store's field (Google Play "What's new" caps at
**500 characters**; Apple's "What's New in This Version" is longer but keep it tight).

---

### Google Play — "What's new" (primary, ~430 chars)

```
jayobee helps you find work that actually fits — no résumé, no quizzes.

Play a quick game: pick the job you'd rather do, over and over, and jayobee surfaces the career preferences you didn't know you had. Then explore 900+ careers one trait at a time, see real median salaries, and get a plain-English "why it fits you" for every match.

100% private — everything stays on your phone. No accounts, no ads, nothing collected.
```

### Punchier alternative (~250 chars)

```
Find work that actually fits. Play a simple game — pick the job you'd rather do — and jayobee learns your hidden career preferences. Explore 900+ careers, see real salaries, and see why each one fits you. Fully private: nothing ever leaves your phone.
```

### Apple App Store — "What's New in This Version" (v1.0)

```
Welcome to jayobee!

Discover careers that fit you by playing a simple game: pick the job you'd rather do, again and again, and jayobee surfaces the preferences you didn't know you had.

• Discover — a quick "this or that" game that learns what you value
• Explore — wander 900+ careers one trait at a time (more artistic, less social, more hands-on)
• Real median salaries from federal data, with sort and filter
• A plain-English "why it fits you" for every match
• Export your results as a PDF

100% private — everything stays on your device. No accounts, no ads, no data collected.
```
