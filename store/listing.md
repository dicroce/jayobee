### Full description (max 4000)
```
Not sure what career fits you? Most tools hand you a long personality quiz and a vague result. jayobee does something different and more fun.

You play a simple game: jayobee shows you two jobs, and you pick the one you'd rather do. Do that a few dozen times and it surfaces the career preferences you didn't even know you had.

Then it shows you where you fit.

DISCOVER - the game
Pick the job you'd rather do, over and over. jayobee learns what you actually value - creative vs. analytical, hands-on vs. abstract, people vs. solitude - and ranks 900+ real careers by how well they fit you. No résumé, no essays, no 100-question quiz.

EXPLORE - wander the map
Start on any job and walk the career landscape one trait at a time: "more artistic," "less social," "more hands-on." See how careers connect, and stumble onto ones you'd never have thought to search for.

For every match, you get:
• A real median salary (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics data), with sort and filter
• A plain-English "why it fits you" - the exact traits driving the match, and the trade-offs
• What the work actually involves, and the education it takes
• Related careers to jump to

Built on O*NET occupational data: 900+ careers grounded in real job characteristics, not horoscopes.

100% PRIVATE
No accounts. No ads. No tracking. jayobee collects nothing and works entirely offline - your choices and results never leave your phone. Export your results as a PDF anytime and share them yourself.

DATA & SOURCES
Occupational information comes from O*NET (https://www.onetcenter.org), sponsored by the U.S. Department of Labor. Wage figures come from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics (https://www.bls.gov/oes/).

jayobee is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing the U.S. Department of Labor, the Bureau of Labor Statistics, or any other government agency.

Whether you're choosing a first career, weighing a change, or just curious what fits you - start playing.
```

> **Note:** The DATA & SOURCES section + disclaimer above is required to pass Google Play's Misleading Claims policy (apps surfacing government data must link sources and disclaim government affiliation). Apply the same block to the Apple description too.
